# Basketball assets

Downloaded October 17, 2026. Both GLBs are bundled locally; gameplay needs no third-party requests.

## ball.glb

"Basketball" by **Poly by Google**, via [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza/m/eopD_12UuB8).
Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Original file: https://static.poly.pizza/e2534fbc-cb90-4b28-bb8e-4f6706957b21.glb
Changes: runtime centering and scaling to gameplay size; mesh and materials unchanged.

## hoop.glb

"Freestanding basketball hoop — Sports equipment" by **Innerscene**.
Source: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/freestanding-basketball-hoop-sports-equipment-33cc5141
Licensed under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Changes: runtime orientation and backboard material adjustments; the original ring/net are replaced with a proportionate ring, mounting bracket and tapered woven net, with score animation.

Court markings, scoreboard, trajectory guide, and gameplay are authored in this project.
