# Ascent sound design

The rocket-booster recording is CC0 1.0 Universal. The charge and launch whoosh were removed.

- booster: NickTimesAudio — https://freesound.org/people/NickTimesAudio/sounds/494827/

Changes: stereo filtering, loudness matching, accelerating booster resampling, envelope fades, soft peak limiting and AAC encoding. The mastered timeline runs from survival phase 480 to 494 seconds. Source hashes are in sources.json.
