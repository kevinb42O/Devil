photographic PBR textures, not generated assets.

The glTF meshes and their 1K diffuse, normal, and packed material textures are
stored locally, so runtime loading does not depend on an external service.
Architecture modules are cloned, positioned and scaled for the courtyard;
rock-face instances form its surrounding cliffs and debris.
