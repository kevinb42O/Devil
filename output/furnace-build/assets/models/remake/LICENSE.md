The source GLBs are unmodified. The game samples their idle/flying pose, bakes
shared geometry, packs material colors into palette UVs, normalizes/scales the
models, and adds procedural movement and independently authored attachments.
The three spawner towers (Rift Obelisk, Twin Foundry, Crown Citadel),
arena tiles, pickups, capsule shells, convoy segments, spires, rings,
weak-point crystals, and gore fragment geometry are authored for this project.

Exact download URLs, SHA-256 hashes, file sizes, and acquisition dates are in
`assets/provenance/remake/models.json` in the repository.

The retained Devil Daggers bullets are a separate exception;
this CC0 notice does not apply to them.
